## What this website does (and doesn't) do

This website is static. It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, contact forms or embedded third-party content, and it loads no external fonts or scripts.

## Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit the site, the hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time, the requested page and your browser type in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure. We have no access to these logs and no influence on how long the provider stores them. Your data may be processed in the USA; the provider is subject to its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contacting us

If you email or call us, we process the data you send us (e.g. name, email address, message) to answer your request. We delete it when it is no longer needed and no retention duty applies.

You can ask us for information about, correction or deletion of your personal data, and object to its processing, by emailing [hello@lupiq.com](mailto:hello@lupiq.com). You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
